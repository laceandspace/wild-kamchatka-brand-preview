About us

Wild Kamchatka brings wild-caught caviar, crab and fish from Kamchatka and the northern seas to Bali. No aquaculture: this fish grew up in the ocean, not in a pen.

The catch is shock-frozen near the fishing grounds, travels in a reefer and sits in our freezers on the island. That chain stays unbroken to your door — which is why the caviar tastes the way Kamchatka knows it.

Ordering is simple: pick on the site, a manager confirms availability in WhatsApp or Telegram and delivers. You pay when the order is confirmed — whichever way suits you.
